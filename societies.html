OUR SOCIETIES

Discover the vibrant communities and ministries that bring our parish family together. Join us in faith, service, and fellowship.

Browse Societies

Filter by category to find communities that resonate with you

Liturgical

Legion of Mary

A lay Catholic association dedicated to spiritual growth through prayer and apostolic work. Members gather weekly to pray the rosary and undertake parish outreach activities.

Charity

Society of St. Vincent de Paul

A voluntary organization committed to combating poverty and supporting those in need within our parish community through home visits and material assistance.

Youth

Young Catholic Students (YCS)

Empowering young people to live their faith boldly through fellowship, faith formation, and community service. Open to all youth aged 16-35 in the parish.

Music

St. Lazarus Parish Choir

The voice of our parish community! We enhance the liturgical experience through sacred music, hymns, and chant at all major masses and liturgical celebrations.

Liturgical

Altar Servers Guild

Young parishioners who assist priests during the Holy Mass and other liturgical services. They receive training in liturgical practices and spiritual formation.
