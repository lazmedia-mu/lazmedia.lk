Convent School

Bethany Holy Angels' Convent

A premier Catholic educational institution nurturing young minds in faith and knowledge

35+ Staff Members Serving Since 1978 800+ Students

Our Mission

Bethany Holy Angels' Convent is committed to providing quality education that integrates academic excellence with spiritual formation. We strive to create an environment where students grow in wisdom, faith, and service to others.

What We Offer

  • Comprehensive curriculum from Kindergarten to Advanced Levels
  • Extracurricular activities including sports, arts, and music
  • Spiritual formation through daily prayers and religious education
  • Community service projects that foster social responsibility
  • Modern facilities including science labs and computer centers

Faith Formation

St. Lazarus' Sunday School

Guiding the youth of our parish in the teachings of Jesus Christ and Catholic traditions

25 Teachers Founded 1955 150+ Students

Our Mission

St. Lazarus' Sunday School is dedicated to nurturing the faith of our young parishioners. Through engaging lessons, prayer, and community activities, we help children and teenagers develop a deep, personal relationship with God.

What We Offer

  • Age-appropriate religious education classes
  • Bible study and scripture exploration
  • Preparation for sacraments: First Communion & Confirmation
  • Youth fellowship and social activities
  • Annual retreats and faith camps
  • Opportunities for youth leadership in parish ministries

Schedule

When: Every Sunday, 9:00 AM - 10:30 AM

Location: Parish Community Hall, St. Lazarus' Church

Contact: Sunday School Coordinator - +94 77 123 4567
