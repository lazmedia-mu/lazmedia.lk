About Our Parish

Est. 1926 • Kurukulawa, Sri Lanka

A Century of Faith in Kurukulawa

St. Lazarus' Church has been a spiritual home for generations of faithful families in Kurukulawa, Ragama since its founding in 1926. Under the Archdiocese of Colombo, our parish serves over 3,000 Catholic families, nurturing deep Christian values and vibrant community life.

Nestled in a serene environment, we continue to preserve rich liturgical traditions while growing as a modern parish community. Our patron saint, St. Lazarus of Bethany, inspires us to live out our faith through service, prayer, and compassion for all.
