Centennial Celebrations

Event Schedule

Join us for a series of special events throughout our centennial jubilee year, celebrating 100 years of faith and community.

02 SEP 2026
Cultural Events

Grand Re-Open of the Church

Join us for the grand re-open of our church after months of renovations and restoration works, celebrating 100 years of faith and community.

5:00 PM On the church premises

After months of dedicated effort and renovation, St. Lazarus’ Church is delighted to announce the Grand Reopening of our beautifully renewed church. Join us for this special celebration on 02nd September 2026 at 5.00 PM, with His Lordship Rt. Rev. Dr. Anton Ranjith, Auxiliary Bishop of Colombo, as the Chief Guest, as we begin a new chapter of faith, prayer, and unity.

All parishioners welcome Live streaming not available
03 SEP 2026
Cultural Events

Hoisting of the flagstaff

Join us for the ceremonial hoisting of the flagstaff at our parish community according to the traditional procedure of preparation for the feast.

5:30 PM Front of the church

Join us on 03rd September at 5:30 PM for the ceremonial hoisting of the flagstaff, marking the beginning of our parish community’s traditional preparation for the feast. Let us come together in faith and unity as we begin this special time of celebration and devotion.

All parishioners welcome Blessing and prayer

Religious Services

Daily Mass and Novena

Join us for the daily Mass and Novena at our parish community.

7:00 PM Inside the church

All parishioners welcome Blessing with St. Lazarus' Statue
